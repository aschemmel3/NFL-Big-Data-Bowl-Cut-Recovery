# Available slant, in-route, burst, and press-related measurements

Inventory checked against local files. Reproduce rookie game-route counts with `./audit_wr_route_options.ps1`; drill coverage comes from `reports/wr_drill_coverage.csv`. These are availability counts, not extracted or validated features.

- Combine slants: `SLANT_ROUTE_LEFT` covers all 106 WRs (38/31/37 by 2023/2024/2025). This is 109 attempts under the original coverage audit. Development tracking has 72 attempts from 69 players. Absence of a 45-degree/0.5-second detection does not make those trajectories unusable: turn-relative speed retention and initial acceleration can be measured with a different segmentation rule.
- Combine deep-in: there is no explicit `DEEP_IN` or `DIG` drill label in the WR coverage inventory. `DAGGER_ROUTE_LEFT` covers all 106 WRs, but its drill name alone does not establish an isolated deep-in cut. Typical inspected development paths show a broad bend. Review trajectory depth and geometry before assigning a deep-in interpretation.
- Rookie NFL routes: `SLANT` has 500/307/218 eligible routes across 2023/2024/2025 (1,025 total); `IN` has 807/593/553 (1,953 total). `IN` does not specify deep versus shallow. These counts use rookie REG routes with no nullifying penalty, including routes without separation. Break depth would require snap-relative receiver tracking and longitudinal displacement normalized for play direction. Pass length is not a substitute for receiver break depth, particularly on untargeted routes.
- Initial burst: x/y, timestamps, and reported speed support movement-onset-relative speed gains, time to a sustained speed threshold, and distance traveled in the next 0.5/1.0 seconds. Time since recording start is not necessarily time since athlete movement onset. Threshold crossings should remain censored if the clip ends early. The supplied 10-yard split exists for 96/106 WRs (34/29/33 by class); vertical and broad jumps are supplementary explosiveness measures rather than direct route burst.
- Post-cut burst: the current prototype already exports signed speed gain and mean acceleration over 0.5 seconds after the speed minimum. Comparing players will require entry-speed, turn-angle, and drill controls. Distinguish this from complete recovery to an earlier speed.
- Press releases: no explicit press, jam/contact, release-technique, or release-win field is supplied, and no WR combine drill is labeled as a defended press-release task. NFL player_play includes pre-snap cushion, team man/zone coverage, and motion status. Small cushion plus man coverage and no motion can define a close-alignment proxy, but it is not verified press contact. Thresholds such as 1 or 2 yards are analysis choices, not provided press labels. The game-tracking cohort is incomplete, so an absent tracked defender cannot establish an uncontested release.

Suggested feature development order: retain comeback speed loss and early recovery; add initial burst from go/slant trajectories; develop slant-specific turn segmentation; then inspect dagger break depth. Treat any close-alignment NFL comparison as exploratory until verified press labels or adequate defender observations are available. Feature construction should continue on 2023–2024 development tracking.
